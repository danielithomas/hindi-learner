# Past Tense with Karna (करना) — To Do/Work

**Date:** 2026-02-19
**Topic:** Using "kaam karna" (to work) in different tenses, the ergative case with "maine", and self-correcting tense mistakes
**Context:** Practising how to describe completed work — moving from present/future to past tense, and learning why "mein" becomes "maine" with "kiya".

---

## The Practice

**Learner's attempts and corrections:**

1. ❌ "kaam par hain" → ✅ "kaam **kar raha hoon**" (I am working)
2. ❌ "kaam tha" → ✅ "kaam **kar raha tha**" (I was working)
3. ❌ "Kya mein bolta kaam the" → ✅ "Kya mein sahi **bol raha tha**?" (Was I saying it correctly?)
4. ✅ "Aaj mein kaam kiya" → 💎 "Aaj **maine** kaam kiya" (Today I worked)

## Core Pattern: Kaam + Karna Across Tenses

In Hindi, "kaam" (work) is a noun. To turn it into a verb ("to work"), pair it with **karna** (to do):

| Hindi | Romanised | English | Tense | Sound |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| काम करूँगा | kaam karunga | I will work | Future | [🔊](https://translate.google.com/?sl=hi&tl=en&text=%E0%A4%95%E0%A4%BE%E0%A4%AE%20%E0%A4%95%E0%A4%B0%E0%A5%82%E0%A4%81%E0%A4%97%E0%A4%BE&op=translate) |
| काम कर रहा हूँ | kaam kar raha hoon | I am working | Present continuous | [🔊](https://translate.google.com/?sl=hi&tl=en&text=%E0%A4%95%E0%A4%BE%E0%A4%AE%20%E0%A4%95%E0%A4%B0%20%E0%A4%B0%E0%A4%B9%E0%A4%BE%20%E0%A4%B9%E0%A5%82%E0%A4%81&op=translate) |
| काम कर रहा था | kaam kar raha tha | I was working | Past continuous | [🔊](https://translate.google.com/?sl=hi&tl=en&text=%E0%A4%95%E0%A4%BE%E0%A4%AE%20%E0%A4%95%E0%A4%B0%20%E0%A4%B0%E0%A4%B9%E0%A4%BE%20%E0%A4%A5%E0%A4%BE&op=translate) |

## The Ergative Case: Mein → Maine

When using **kiya** (did) in the past tense, the subject changes from **mein** (I) to **maine** (मैंने):

| Subject | Verb | Example | Sound |
|---|---|---|---|
| मैं (mein) | kar raha hoon | Mein kaam kar raha hoon (I am working) | [🔊](https://translate.google.com/?sl=hi&tl=en&text=%E0%A4%AE%E0%A5%88%E0%A4%82%20%E0%A4%95%E0%A4%BE%E0%A4%AE%20%E0%A4%95%E0%A4%B0%20%E0%A4%B0%E0%A4%B9%E0%A4%BE%20%E0%A4%B9%E0%A5%82%E0%A4%81&op=translate) |
| मैंने (maine) | kiya | Maine kaam kiya (I did work) | [🔊](https://translate.google.com/?sl=hi&tl=en&text=%E0%A4%AE%E0%A5%88%E0%A4%82%E0%A4%A8%E0%A5%87%20%E0%A4%95%E0%A4%BE%E0%A4%AE%20%E0%A4%95%E0%A4%BF%E0%A4%AF%E0%A4%BE&op=translate) |

**Rule of thumb:**
- Present/future → **mein**
- Past tense with "kiya" (or any transitive verb in simple past) → **maine**

## Common Mistakes & Fixes

### ❌ "Mein kaam tha"
- Reads as: "I was work" (kaam as noun, no verb)
- ✅ Fix: "Mein kaam **kar raha tha**" (I was working)

### ❌ "Kya mein bolta kaam the"
- Word order and tense mismatch
- ✅ Fix: "Kya mein sahi **bol raha tha**?" (Was I saying it correctly?)
- "bolta" = habitual, "bol raha tha" = was saying (past continuous)
- "the" → "tha" (singular masculine for "I")

### ❌ "Mein kaam karenge"
- "Karenge" = we will do (plural/formal)
- ✅ For informal "I will do": "Mein kaam kar**unga**" (masculine)
- Note: "karenge" is acceptable in formal/professional Hindi for "I"

**Key takeaways:**
- **Karna** (करना) is one of the most important verbs in Hindi — it turns nouns into actions (kaam karna = to work, madad karna = to help, pyaar karna = to love)
- The ergative **maine** pattern applies whenever you use a transitive verb in simple past — not just kiya
- The moment you say "kiya", switch "mein" → "maine"
