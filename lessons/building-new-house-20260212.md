## Overview

| Word (Hindi) | Romanised | Meaning | Sound |
|--------------|-----------|---------|-------|
| जल्दी | jaldi | soon / quickly | [🔊 jaldi](https://translate.google.com/?sl=hi&tl=en&text=%E0%A4%9C%E0%A4%B2%E0%A5%8D%E0%A4%A6%E0%A5%80&op=translate) |

**Note:** जल्दी can mean both "soon" and "quickly" depending on context:
- जल्दी आना = Come quickly / Come soon
- जल्दी होगा = It will happen soon

## Part 6: Common Error Correction

### Original Attempt:
> "Hum ghar ko banega. Jadi hain."

### Corrections:

| Issue | Original | Corrected | Explanation |
|-------|----------|-----------|-------------|
| Verb conjugation | banega | banayenge | With "hum" (we), use plural future: बनाएँगे |
| Unnecessary postposition | ghar ko | ghar | No को needed here |
| Spelling | jadi | jaldi | Correct spelling is जल्दी (jaldi) |
| Unnecessary verb | jaldi hain | jaldi | Just जल्दी alone works for "soon" |

### Correct Version:
| Hindi | Romanised | Meaning | Sound |
|-------|-----------|---------|-------|
| हम घर बनाएँगे। जल्दी! | Hum ghar banayenge. Jaldi! | We will build a house. Soon! | [🔊 Hum ghar banayenge jaldi](https://translate.google.com/?sl=hi&tl=en&text=%E0%A4%B9%E0%A4%AE%20%E0%A4%98%E0%A4%B0%20%E0%A4%AC%E0%A4%A8%E0%A4%BE%E0%A4%8F%E0%A4%81%E0%A4%97%E0%A5%87%E0%A5%A4%20%E0%A4%9C%E0%A4%B2%E0%A5%8D%E0%A4%A6%E0%A5%80!&op=translate) |

## Part 7: Bonus Phrase

| Phrase (Hindi) | Romanised | Meaning | Sound |
|----------------|-----------|---------|-------|
| सपनों का घर जल्दी बनेगा! | Sapno ka ghar jaldi banega! | The dream house will be built soon! | [🔊 Sapno ka ghar jaldi banega](https://translate.google.com/?sl=hi&tl=en&text=%E0%A4%B8%E0%A4%AA%E0%A4%A8%E0%A5%8B%E0%A4%82%20%E0%A4%95%E0%A4%BE%20%E0%A4%98%E0%A4%B0%20%E0%A4%9C%E0%A4%B2%E0%A5%8D%E0%A4%A6%E0%A5%80%20%E0%A4%AC%E0%A4%A8%E0%A5%87%E0%A4%97%E0%A4%BE!&op=translate) |

**Note:** Here we use बनेगा (banega - will be built) because the house is the subject. Compare:
- हम घर **बनाएँगे** = We will build a house (active - we are doing it)
- घर **बनेगा** = The house will be built (passive/intransitive)

## Summary: Key Takeaways

1. **House vocabulary**: घर (ghar), नया घर (naya ghar), सपनों का घर (sapno ka ghar)
2. **Building verb**: बनाना (banana) → हम बनाएँगे (hum banayenge - we will build)
3. **Time**: जल्दी (jaldi) means "soon" - no extra verb needed!
4. **Verb agreement**: Match your verb ending to the subject (हम → -एँगे ending)
5. **Feelings**: कैसा लग रहा है? (kaisa lag raha hai?) = How does it feel?
