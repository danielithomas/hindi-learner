# Teamwork & Projects — Working Together

**Date:** 2026-02-17
**Topic:** Talking about working on a project with someone else
**Context:** Practising how to describe collaborative work — doing a project together, using the correct verb conjugation for "we" vs "I".

---

## The Practice

**Learner's attempt:**
> "Mein aur [partner] ko [Project] kam karonga"

**Corrected version:**
> "Mein aur [partner] **Project par kaam karenge**"

---

## Key Corrections

### 1. करूँगा (karunga) vs करेंगे (karenge)

When you're doing something **alone**, use **करूँगा / karunga** (I will do):
> Mein kaam **karunga** — I will work

When you're doing something **with someone else** (we), use **करेंगे / karenge** (we will do):
> Hum kaam **karenge** — We will work

| Hindi | Romanised | Meaning | Sound |
|---|---|---|---|
| करूँगा | karunga | I will do (male) | [🔊 karunga](https://translate.google.com/?sl=hi&tl=en&text=%E0%A4%95%E0%A4%B0%E0%A5%82%E0%A4%81%E0%A4%97%E0%A4%BE&op=translate) |
| करूँगी | karungi | I will do (female) | [🔊 karungi](https://translate.google.com/?sl=hi&tl=en&text=%E0%A4%95%E0%A4%B0%E0%A5%82%E0%A4%81%E0%A4%97%E0%A5%80&op=translate) |
| करेंगे | karenge | we will do | [🔊 karenge](https://translate.google.com/?sl=hi&tl=en&text=%E0%A4%95%E0%A4%B0%E0%A5%87%E0%A4%82%E0%A4%97%E0%A5%87&op=translate) |

**Rule:** "Mein aur ___" = **we** → always use **हम (hum)** verb forms (करेंगे, जाएँगे, खाएँगे, etc.)

### 2. पर (par) — "on" a project

Use **पर (par)** when working **on** something:
> Project **par** kaam karna — To work **on** a project

Don't use **को (ko)** here — को is for objects/people receiving an action, not for what you're working on.

| Hindi | Romanised | Meaning | Sound |
|---|---|---|---|
| पर | par | on | [🔊 par](https://translate.google.com/?sl=hi&tl=en&text=%E0%A4%AA%E0%A4%B0&op=translate) |

### 3. काम (kaam) vs कम (kam)

These sound similar but mean very different things!

| Hindi | Romanised | Meaning | Sound |
|---|---|---|---|
| काम | kaam | work | [🔊 kaam](https://translate.google.com/?sl=hi&tl=en&text=%E0%A4%95%E0%A4%BE%E0%A4%AE&op=translate) |
| कम | kam | less / fewer | [🔊 kam](https://translate.google.com/?sl=hi&tl=en&text=%E0%A4%95%E0%A4%AE&op=translate) |

The difference is the long **aa** vowel. **काम** (kaam) has a longer "aa" sound. Listen to both!

## Practice Exercises

Try saying these out loud:

1. "I will work on the project" → Mein project par kaam **karunga**
2. "We will work on the project" → Hum project par kaam **karenge**
3. "We will work together" → Hum saath milkar kaam **karenge**
4. "Are you tired?" → Kya aapko thakaan lag rahi hai?
5. "There is less work today" → Aaj **kam** kaam hai (less work — notice both kam and kaam!)

---

## Grammar Summary: "I will" vs "We will"

| Subject | Verb Form | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Mein (I, male) | करूँगा karunga | Mein kaam karunga |
| Mein (I, female) | करूँगी karungi | Mein kaam karungi |
| Hum (We) | करेंगे karenge | Hum kaam karenge |
| Mein aur ___ (I and ___) | करेंगे karenge | Mein aur dost kaam karenge |

**Key takeaway:** The moment you add "aur [someone]" after "mein", the verb switches to the **hum** (we) form!
