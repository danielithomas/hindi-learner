## Grammar Notes

### "Mujhe pasand aaya" (I liked)
In Hindi, liking uses **mujhe** (to me) not **mein** (I). The thing you like is the subject:
- ✅ **Mujhe ghar pasand aaya** — I liked the house (lit. "To me, the house came to liking")
- ❌ ~~Mein ghar pasand aaya~~

### Past tense with "ne"
When using transitive verbs in past tense, Hindi uses **maine** (I + ne):
- **Maine ghar dekha** — I saw the house
- **Maine khana khaya** — I ate food

### Singular vs plural "hai"
- **Vo ghar accha hai** — That house is good (singular = hai)
- **Vo ghar acche hain** — Those houses are good (plural = hain)
