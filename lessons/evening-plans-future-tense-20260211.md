## Overview

This lesson emerged from a real conversation about a busy day at work and evening plans to rest. You'll learn how to talk about completed work, express future intentions, and master the crucial difference between "I will do" and "you will do" in Hindi.

## Part 1: Talking About Completed Work

### Key Phrase: "A lot of work got done today"

| Hindi | Romanised | Meaning | Sound |
|-------|-----------|---------|-------|
| आज बहुत काम हो गया | aaj bahut kaam ho gaya | Today a lot of work got done | [🔊 aaj bahut kaam ho gaya](https://translate.google.com/?sl=hi&tl=en&text=%E0%A4%86%E0%A4%9C%20%E0%A4%AC%E0%A4%B9%E0%A5%81%E0%A4%A4%20%E0%A4%95%E0%A4%BE%E0%A4%AE%20%E0%A4%B9%E0%A5%8B%20%E0%A4%97%E0%A4%AF%E0%A4%BE&op=translate) |

### Breaking it down:

| Word | Romanised | Meaning | Sound |
|------|-----------|---------|-------|
| आज | aaj | today | [🔊 aaj](https://translate.google.com/?sl=hi&tl=en&text=%E0%A4%86%E0%A4%9C&op=translate) |
| काम | kaam | work | [🔊 kaam](https://translate.google.com/?sl=hi&tl=en&text=%E0%A4%95%E0%A4%BE%E0%A4%AE&op=translate) |
| हो गया | ho gaya | happened/got done | [🔊 ho gaya](https://translate.google.com/?sl=hi&tl=en&text=%E0%A4%B9%E0%A5%8B%20%E0%A4%97%E0%A4%AF%E0%A4%BE&op=translate) |

**Grammar Note:** The construction "हो गया" (ho gaya) indicates completion. It's like saying something "got done" or "happened." This is the **completive aspect** in Hindi.

- काम हो गया = Work got done (passive/impersonal)
- काम किया = (I) did work (active)

## Part 4: Future Tense - The Critical Distinction

This is where many learners make mistakes! Pay close attention to who is doing the action:

### करना (karna) - "to do" - Future Tense Conjugation

| Person | Hindi | Romanised | Meaning | Sound |
|--------|-------|-----------|---------|-------|
| I (male) | करूँगा | karunga | I will do | [🔊 karunga](https://translate.google.com/?sl=hi&tl=en&text=%E0%A4%95%E0%A4%B0%E0%A5%82%E0%A4%81%E0%A4%97%E0%A4%BE&op=translate) |
| I (female) | करूँगी | karungi | I will do | [🔊 karungi](https://translate.google.com/?sl=hi&tl=en&text=%E0%A4%95%E0%A4%B0%E0%A5%82%E0%A4%81%E0%A4%97%E0%A5%80&op=translate) |
| You (informal) | करोगे | karoge | You will do (m) | [🔊 karoge](https://translate.google.com/?sl=hi&tl=en&text=%E0%A4%95%E0%A4%B0%E0%A5%8B%E0%A4%97%E0%A5%87&op=translate) |
| You (informal, f) | करोगी | karogi | You will do (f) | [🔊 karogi](https://translate.google.com/?sl=hi&tl=en&text=%E0%A4%95%E0%A4%B0%E0%A5%8B%E0%A4%97%E0%A5%80&op=translate) |
| You (formal) | करेंगे | karenge | You will do | [🔊 karenge](https://translate.google.com/?sl=hi&tl=en&text=%E0%A4%95%E0%A4%B0%E0%A5%87%E0%A4%82%E0%A4%97%E0%A5%87&op=translate) |

### ⚠️ Common Mistake Alert!

**Wrong:** "Mere plan kuch bahut **karoge** hain" ❌  
(Using "you will do" when meaning "I will do")

**Correct:** "Aaj shaam bahut aaram **karunga**" ✓  
(Using "I will do" correctly)

### Memory Trick:
- कर**ूँगा** (kar**unga**) = **I** will do — Think: "**U**nga = m**U**jhe (me)"
- कर**ोगे** (kar**oge**) = **You** will do — Think: "**O**ge = t**U**m (you)"

## Part 6: Greetings - Good Night

| Hindi | Romanised | Meaning | Sound |
|-------|-----------|---------|-------|
| शुभ रात्रि | shubh raatri | Good night (formal) | [🔊 shubh raatri](https://translate.google.com/?sl=hi&tl=en&text=%E0%A4%B6%E0%A5%81%E0%A4%AD%20%E0%A4%B0%E0%A4%BE%E0%A4%A4%E0%A5%8D%E0%A4%B0%E0%A4%BF&op=translate) |

**Note:** शुभ (shubh) means "auspicious" or "good" — you'll see this in many well-wishes:
- शुभ प्रभात (shubh prabhaat) = Good morning
- शुभ रात्रि (shubh raatri) = Good night

## Practice Exercises

### Exercise 1: Fill in the blanks with the correct future tense

1. मैं कल काम _______ (I will do work tomorrow)
2. तुम क्या _______ ? (What will you do?)
3. आज शाम मैं आराम _______ (Tonight I will rest)

**Answers:**
1. करूँगा (karunga)
2. करोगे (karoge)
3. करूँगा (karunga)

### Exercise 2: Translate to Hindi

1. "Today a lot of work got done" → _______________
2. "Tonight I will rest a lot" → _______________
3. "Good night" → _______________

**Answers:**
1. आज बहुत काम हो गया (aaj bahut kaam ho gaya)
2. आज शाम बहुत आराम करूँगा (aaj shaam bahut aaram karunga)
3. शुभ रात्रि (shubh raatri)
